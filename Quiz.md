1. Why is a skeleton insufficient for body-shape-conditioned motion?
   - A. It cannot represent any temporal information.
   - B. It omits proportions and mass-related differences that influence movement.

   - A. Retrieve an initial configuration, then refine it against the observation.
   - B. Generate audio, then classify the subject.
   - C. Replace depth with color-only segmentation.

3. What does SIMSPINE contribute?
   - A. A clinical diagnostic device.
   - B. A photorealistic clothing simulator.
   - C. Simulation-derived vertebral annotations and benchmark baselines.

4. When is multi-view capture especially attractive?
   - A. When calibration is impossible.
   - B. When richer 3D surface geometry justifies extra equipment.
   - C. When the subject must be invisible to all sensors.

5. What is the safest interpretation of a posture estimate?
   - A. It is an estimate whose validity depends on task, assumptions, and validation.
   - B. It is automatically a medical diagnosis.
   - C. It is ground truth because it is three-dimensional.

**Answer key:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-C, 4-B, 5-A.
